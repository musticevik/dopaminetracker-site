There are many apps that block social media, and they stop you in different ways. Some block the app outright, some make you wait before it opens, and one needs a physical device. This page puts nine of them in one table.

We make Neurofast, which is one of the nine. The facts about the other apps come from their own websites and store listings, checked on 25 November 2026. Prices are US prices and vary by country.

The apps one by one

Neurofast

Neurofast blocks an app when its daily limit is reached, and the block holds until midnight. Lifting it early is free on Pro and costs a one-time fee without it. With Smart block it removes Reels, Shorts and TikTok's feeds and leaves messages working, on both iPhone and Android. It has no computer app. Details are in What is Neurofast?

Opal

Opal blocks apps through sessions, schedules and limits on iPhone, Android and Mac. Its Hard Mode is for people who want no way out. Its yearly plan is the most expensive on this list. We compare it in detail in Neurofast vs Opal.

ScreenZen

ScreenZen is free and runs on phones and computers. It makes you wait before an app opens and can lengthen the wait with each open. It suits people for whom a pause is enough. See Neurofast vs ScreenZen.

one sec

one sec shows a breathing exercise each time you open a chosen app. Its maker says it delays access "instead of blocking apps completely". On Android it can remove Reels and Shorts; its own help pages say this is not possible on iPhone.

AppBlock

AppBlock blocks apps, websites and keywords on schedules that can follow time, place or Wi-Fi. On Android it can block Reels, Stories and Shorts; on iPhone it says it cannot.

Freedom

Freedom blocks apps and websites across phones and computers at the same time. It is the choice if your distraction follows you from the phone to the laptop.

Jomo

Jomo is for Apple devices only. It blocks apps at set times, after a limit, or until you complete an action.

Brick

Brick is a physical device. You tap your phone on it to block your apps and tap again to unblock them, so the key can stay in another room. There is no subscription, but you have to buy the device.

BePresent

BePresent builds blocking sessions around goals, streaks and leaderboards with friends.

How to choose

  • A pause is enough for you: ScreenZen (free) or one sec.
  • You want Reels and Shorts gone and your messages kept: Neurofast on iPhone and Android; AppBlock or one sec on Android; ScreenZen's Minimal mode on iPhone.
  • You want the same blocks on your computer: Freedom or ScreenZen on Mac and Windows, or Opal on a Mac.
  • You want the key out of reach: Brick.
  • You want to do it with friends: BePresent.
  • You want a block that costs something to lift: Neurofast.

If Neurofast sounds right, it is free to start on the App Store and on Google Play.
