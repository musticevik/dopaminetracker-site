FAQ

Common questions

Is app blocking free?

Yes. A daily limit per app and closing an app completely on chosen days are free for everyone. A block lasts until midnight.

What does Pro add?

Unlocking a block for today or loosening a limit, a strict focus mode that cannot be cancelled, and an unbreakable block that cannot be lifted even on Pro.

Where does my screen time data go?

Per-app usage never leaves your phone. Only your onboarding answers and subscription status sync with your account.

Which permissions are needed?

Usage access to measure screen time; display over other apps so the block screen can appear. Optional Do Not Disturb access to silence notifications during focus sessions.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android only for now. App blocking relies on Android's usage access API.
