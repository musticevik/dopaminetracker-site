How it works

Three steps.

See the real number

Grant usage access and see your true per-app screen time on the first launch.

Set a limit

Pick the app that pulls you back and choose a daily limit, closed days or a routine such as Deep work or Wind down. Setting a block is free.

The block holds

Once the limit is reached the app does not open until midnight. Opening it early is free on Pro and a small one-time fee without it.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Neurofast?

Neurofast is a social media blocker and screen time tracker for iPhone and Android. It shows how long you spend in each app and blocks the ones you choose once you reach your limit. It was called Dopamine Tracker until November 2026.

Is app blocking free?

Yes. A daily limit per app, closing an app completely on chosen days, quick block and one routine are free. On iPhone the free plan blocks one app at a time. A daily limit holds until midnight.

What does Pro add?

Smart block for Reels, Shorts and TikTok's feeds, opening a block for today or loosening a limit without a fee, strict focus sessions, unbreakable blocks, several routines at once and a short wait before an app opens.

Where does my screen time data go?

Per-app usage never leaves your phone. Only your onboarding answers and subscription status sync with your account.

Which permissions are needed?

On Android: usage access to measure screen time, and display over other apps so the block screen can appear. Do Not Disturb access is optional, and Smart block asks for accessibility access only if you turn it on. On iPhone: Screen Time access.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. Neurofast is on the App Store for iPhone (iOS 16 or later) and on Google Play for Android. On iPhone, blocks run on Apple's Screen Time and the free plan blocks one app at a time.

Can it block Reels and Shorts without blocking the whole app?

Yes, with Smart block (Pro). On Android it blocks Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok's feeds while messages and regular videos keep working. On iPhone, Neurofast opens Instagram, YouTube and TikTok in its own browser with those feeds left out.

How is Neurofast different from Opal or ScreenZen?

Opal, ScreenZen, one sec and AppBlock also block distracting apps. Neurofast costs less than Opal, blocks Reels and Shorts on both iPhone and Android, and makes a block cost something to lift. The details are in Neurofast vs Opal, Neurofast vs ScreenZen and the best social media blocker apps compared.
