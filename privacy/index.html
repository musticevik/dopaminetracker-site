Privacy policy

Your data: where, how long, why

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Neurofast ("the app", formerly Dopamine Tracker) is a screen time tracker and app blocker for Android and iOS. Sections 1 to 5 describe the Android app; section 6 lists what is different on iPhone. This policy explains which data the app processes, which of it stays on your phone, and how you can delete it.

1. Data that stays on your phone

The following is kept only in the app's database on your device and is never sent to our servers:

  • Per-app usage time (which app you used for how long), read through Android's usage access permission.
  • Your blocking rules: daily limits, closed days, the unbreakable setting, how many times you saw the block screen.
  • Focus sessions, daily goal history and streaks.
  • Your daily dopamine score and its history.
  • Notification preferences, theme and language.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data from your device.

2. Data synced with your account

You can use the app without an account; an anonymous identity is created in that case. If you sign in with email or Google, the following is stored on our servers (Supabase infrastructure, European Union data centre) tied to your account:

  • Account details: your email address, display name, sign-in method.
  • Onboarding answers: why you use the app, your estimated daily usage, your goal.
  • Subscription status: whether Pro is active and when it ends (via RevenueCat).

Usage time, blocking rules and focus sessions are not synced with your account.

3. Notifications and app analytics

These apply whether or not you have an account:

  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, the app registers a notification token and your app language with our servers, so we can send you messages — about updates, and about offers if you keep "Premium suggestions" on. If you are signed in, the token is linked to your account. Turning notifications off in the app deletes it from our servers.
  • App analytics: which screens of the app you open and a few in-app events (for example finishing setup, viewing the Pro screen, the result of a purchase), plus your device model, operating system and app version. This is linked to a random identifier and, if you are signed in, to your account ID — never your email address. It never includes which apps you use or for how long.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase: authentication and account data storage.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing: purchasing and validating subscriptions. Payment details stay with Google Play; the app never sees card data.
  • Google AdMob: the app includes the ads SDK, which may access the device advertising ID. You can reset the ID or opt out of personalisation in Android settings.
  • PostHog: the app analytics described in section 3.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering notifications to your device. They receive the notification token and the message.

Supabase stores account data in the European Union; PostHog, RevenueCat and Expo process data in the United States. These services are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not sell your data to third parties for advertising.

5. Permissions

  • Usage access: required to measure screen time.
  • Display over other apps: to show the block screen when you open an app that has reached its limit.
  • Notifications: weekly progress, evening reminders and in-the-moment nudges; each can be switched off separately in the app. Messages we send from our server use the same permission; offers among them stop when you turn "Premium suggestions" off.
  • Do Not Disturb access: requested only if you choose to silence notifications during focus sessions.
  • Accessibility service (Smart block, optional, Pro): requested only if you turn on Smart block, after an in-app screen that explains it and asks for your consent. When it is on, the service reads what is on screen only inside Instagram, YouTube and web browsers (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox, Edge, Opera, Brave, DuckDuckGo), and only for the parts you turned on. It uses this to recognise Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and, from the browser address bar, adult websites (checked against a list stored in the app), and then takes you back or shows the block screen. Everything is checked on your phone. The screen content, website addresses and app activity it sees are never stored, logged, sent to our servers or shared with anyone. The only thing the app records is a count of how many times each Smart block feature blocked something, and analytics receive only the feature name, whether it is on or allowed, and that it blocked something. It does not read any other app, does not read what you type anywhere except the browser address bar, and does not perform taps or gestures. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in Android's accessibility settings.

6. On iPhone

The iPhone app works differently from the Android app in these ways:

  • Screen Time: the app uses Apple's Screen Time framework (FamilyControls, DeviceActivity, ManagedSettings). When you pick apps in Apple's picker, iOS gives the app opaque tokens, not app names; the app never learns which apps you picked. They are stored only on your device, in the app's shared App Group container. The screen time numbers on the Dashboard and Analytics tabs are drawn by an Apple-sandboxed report extension that cannot send data anywhere. Your usage, the apps you picked, your blocking rules, routines and focus sessions stay on your device.
  • Feed-free browser: the app can open Instagram, YouTube and TikTok in a browser built into the app. The pages are the sites' own web pages; the app only hides some sections with a small script and does not read, store or send what you do there. You sign in on Instagram's, Google's and TikTok's own pages, so we never see your login; the sites' cookies stay in the browser on your device.
  • Purchases: subscriptions and purchases use Apple In-App Purchase, validated through RevenueCat. Payment details stay with Apple.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, the app registers a push token (Apple Push Notification service, delivered through Expo) and your app language with our servers. The app also posts local notifications on your device, for example the one that opens the unlock screen after you tap "Unlock for today" on the block shield.
  • No ads, no tracking: the iPhone app shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Sign in: you can sign in with Apple or with an email one-time code. With Sign in with Apple you may share or hide your email address.
  • Deleting your data: Profile > Delete account removes your account data from our servers. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

7. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and the data we store with it from Profile → Delete my account; deletion is immediate, and your notification token is unlinked from the account. App analytics linked to your account ID are kept by PostHog and deleted on request — email us. On-device data is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Children

The app is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
