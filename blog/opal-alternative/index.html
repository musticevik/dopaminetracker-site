Opal alternative: how Neurofast compares with Opal

November 25, 2026 · 3 min read

Opal is one of the best-known screen time apps. People who look for an alternative usually want a lower price, or a way to block Reels and Shorts without blocking the whole of Instagram and YouTube. This page compares Opal and Neurofast on those points.

We make Neurofast, so every statement about Opal below links to Opal's own pages. Everything was checked on 25 November 2026, and prices are US prices, which vary by country.

Price

Both apps block apps for free. Opal's free plan gives you three rules. Opal Pro costs $99.99 a year or $19.99 a month in the US, with a lifetime option at $399.

Neurofast Pro costs $29.99 a year or $4.99 a month. On Android the free plan has no limit on how many apps you put a daily limit on. On iPhone the free plan blocks one app at a time.

Reels and Shorts

Opal blocks whole apps and websites. When a user asked on Opal's forum about blocking TikTok, Shorts and Reels separately, Opal staff answered in August 2025 that it "isn't currently possible within Apple's framework".

Neurofast takes two routes. On Android, Smart block recognises the Reels and Shorts players and TikTok's feeds inside the apps and takes you back out, while messages keep working. On iPhone it opens Instagram, YouTube and TikTok in its own browser with those feeds left out. Both are part of Pro, and the setup is in our guide to blocking Reels and Shorts.

How strict the block is

Opal's Hard Mode is described on its pricing page as "No way out. You can't turn Opal off." Its help centre adds that you leave a Hard Mode rule with an Emergency Pass.

In Neurofast every block costs something to lift early. Pro members can open a block for the day, and everyone else pays a one-time $1.99. An unbreakable block has no exit at all until it ends.

When Opal is the better choice

  • You work on a Mac and want the same blocks there. Opal has a Mac app and Neurofast does not.
  • You use an iPhone and want several blocks without paying. Opal's free plan has three rules; Neurofast's free plan on iPhone blocks one app at a time.
  • You want the extras around blocking, such as focus timers with soundscapes and the Opal Score.

When Neurofast fits better

  • You want to pay less for the paid plan.
  • You want Reels, Shorts and TikTok's feed gone and your messages left alone.
  • You use Android and want daily limits on many apps for free.
  • You want the app in Turkish as well as English.

Trying Neurofast

  1. Install it from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Look at your real screen time on the first launch.
  3. Put a daily limit on the app that takes most of it. You can keep Opal installed while you compare.
