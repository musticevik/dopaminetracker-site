ScreenZen alternative: how Neurofast compares with ScreenZen

November 25, 2026 · 3 min read

ScreenZen is free, so price is not a reason to leave it. People who look for a ScreenZen alternative usually found that they wait out the pause and open the app anyway. This page compares ScreenZen and Neurofast on how they stop you, on Reels blocking and on platforms.

We make Neurofast, so every statement about ScreenZen below links to ScreenZen's own pages. Everything was checked on 25 November 2026.

A pause or a block

ScreenZen's main tool is a delay. You tap Instagram, ScreenZen asks you to wait, and the wait can get longer each time you open the app that day. Its App Store description also lists strict blocks "for time ranges, after a certain number of opens or ScreenTime".

Neurofast starts from the block. You set a daily limit or a routine, and once it applies the app does not open. Lifting it early is free on Pro and costs a one-time $1.99 without it. Neurofast also has a short wait before an app opens, as a Pro option.

Reels and Shorts

The release notes for ScreenZen 4.0.0 on the App Store (September 2026) describe a mode called Minimal: "use Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit and more without Reels, Shorts or endless feeds, with messages-only options". ScreenZen's website and its Google Play listing did not mention it when we checked, so we could not confirm it on Android.

Neurofast blocks Reels, Shorts and TikTok's feeds on both systems. On Android it works inside the Instagram, YouTube and TikTok apps. On iPhone it opens them in its own browser without those feeds. Both are part of Pro, and the setup is in our guide to blocking Reels and Shorts.

When ScreenZen is the better choice

  • You want everything for free. ScreenZen has no paid plan.
  • You want the same tool on a Mac or a Windows computer. Neurofast is on phones only.
  • A pause is enough to stop you. In ScreenZen the pause is free; in Neurofast it is a Pro option.
  • You want another person to hold the key. ScreenZen lets you add an accountability partner.

When Neurofast fits better

  • You sit through the pause and open the app anyway, and you want a block that stays.
  • You use Android and want Reels, Shorts and TikTok's feed blocked inside the apps.
  • You want ready-made routines such as Deep work, Study mode and Wind down.
  • You want the app in Turkish as well as English.

Trying Neurofast

  1. Install it from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Put a daily limit on the one app you open most. That is free.
  3. Use it for a week next to ScreenZen and keep the one that changes your screen time.
