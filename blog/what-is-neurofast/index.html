What is Neurofast?

November 25, 2026 · 5 min read

Neurofast is an app for iPhone and Android that shows how much time you spend in each app and blocks the ones you choose once you reach the limit you set. It can also block only the short-video feeds of Instagram, YouTube and TikTok, so Reels, Shorts and the For You page are gone and your messages still work.

The app is free to download on the App Store and on Google Play. As of November 2026 more than 300 people use it actively, and together they have saved more than 1,000 hours of screen time.

What Neurofast does

It shows your real screen time

On the first launch Neurofast shows today's screen time for every app on your phone, along with the last days and weeks. Most people find that the total is higher than the number they would have guessed. The analytics screen breaks the time down by day, by week and by category, and points out your heaviest day. On Android the app also turns each day into a score from 0 to 100; on iPhone it counts how often you pick up the phone.

It blocks the apps you choose

You pick an app and set a daily limit, for example 30 minutes of Instagram, or you close it completely on the days you choose. When the limit is reached a block screen takes over, and the app does not open again until midnight. There are also routines with ready-made templates (Deep work, Study mode, Wind down, Dinner time, Morning reset and Weekend reset), a quick block that locks your distracting apps for 15 minutes to 2 hours, and focus sessions with a timer.

It blocks Reels and Shorts and leaves the rest

Smart block is for people who need Instagram for messages or YouTube for long videos and lose their time in the short-video feeds. On Android it blocks Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok's feeds while DMs, stories and regular videos keep working. On iPhone, Neurofast opens Instagram, YouTube and TikTok in its own browser with those feeds left out. Smart block is part of Pro. The setup is explained step by step in How to block Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts without deleting the apps.

Why the block is hard to switch off

Most blockers can be turned off with one tap, and that tap happens at the moment you most want to scroll. In Neurofast, setting a block is free and removing it early costs something: Pro members can open a block for the day, and everyone else pays a small one-time fee ($1.99 in the US). If you want no way out at all, you can set an unbreakable block, which nobody can lift until the day ends, Pro members included.

What is free and what Pro adds

Screen time tracking, daily limits, full-day blocks, quick block, one routine and focus sessions are free. On iPhone the free plan blocks one app at a time.

Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription. It adds:

  • Smart block for Reels, Shorts and TikTok's feeds
  • opening a block for the day or loosening a limit without a fee
  • strict focus sessions that cannot be cancelled
  • unbreakable blocks
  • several routines at once, and a short wait before an app opens
  • on iPhone, blocks on as many apps as you like

In the US App Store, Pro costs $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and new subscribers start with a free trial. The price depends on your country and store and is shown before you subscribe. You can cancel at any time in your store account.

Where your data stays

Your per-app usage, your limits and your focus sessions stay on your phone and are never sent to our servers. If you create an account, it stores your sign-in details, your onboarding answers and your subscription status.

On iPhone, Apple's Screen Time keeps the list of apps you picked private, so Neurofast itself never learns it. On Android, Smart block uses the accessibility service. It reads the screen only inside Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and web browsers, checks everything on the phone and stores nothing. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Who makes it

Neurofast is built by an independent developer. Until November 2026 the app was called Dopamine Tracker. Only the name changed: the app, the accounts and the subscriptions are the same. The app is available in English and Turkish.

Neurofast is a tool for managing your own phone use. It is not a medical product, and it is not a parental control app.

Common questions

Is Neurofast free?

Yes. The app is free to download and blocking apps is free. Pro is optional.

Does Neurofast work on iPhone?

Yes. It needs iOS 16 or later. Blocks run on Apple's Screen Time, so a block holds even when Neurofast is closed.

Can I get around a block I set?

You set it, so you can lift it, but it takes more than a tap. Without Pro, opening a block early costs a small fee, and an unbreakable block stays until the day ends.
