How to block Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts without deleting the apps

November 25, 2026 · 4 min read

You open Instagram to answer a message, and the Reels tab is one tap away. Deleting the app would solve it, but the messages, the group chats and the people you follow would go with it. This guide covers the ways to block Reels and Shorts and keep the rest of the app.

Why the settings in Instagram and YouTube are not enough

At the time of writing (November 2026) we know of no setting in Instagram or YouTube that removes Reels or Shorts for good. Both apps offer time reminders, and a reminder can be dismissed with one tap at the moment you want to keep watching.

What your phone's own limits can do

Android has app timers in Digital Wellbeing and iPhone has App Limits in Screen Time. Both work on the whole app: when the time is up Instagram closes, and your messages close with it.

They are also easy to undo. On iPhone the limit screen offers an "Ignore Limit" button unless someone else holds your Screen Time passcode, and on Android you can delete the timer in settings.

Opening instagram.com or youtube.com in a browser does not help either, because the websites have Reels and Shorts too.

Block only Reels and Shorts on Android

Smart block in Neurofast recognises the Reels and Shorts players and takes you back out of them. Everything else in the app keeps working.

  1. Install Neurofast from Google Play and open it.
  2. Open the Block screen (the shield icon) and find the Smart block section.
  3. Turn on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts or both. Smart block is a Pro feature, so the app asks you to start Pro if you have not.
  4. Read the screen that explains what Smart block reads, then tap Accept and open settings.
  5. In Android's accessibility settings, find Smart block and turn it on.

From then on, opening the Reels tab or a Short sends you back, and a short screen tells you it is blocked. In Instagram your DMs, feed, stories and profiles work as before, and in YouTube regular videos play as before.

If friends send you Reels, switch on Allow Reels from chats. A Reel opened from a chat plays, and swiping to the next one stays blocked.

The same section has a switch for TikTok called TikTok friends only. It blocks the For You, Following and Explore feeds and keeps your messages, the videos people send you, your own profile and posting.

About the accessibility permission

Android gives an app no other way to see which screen of another app is open, which is why Smart block needs the accessibility service. It listens only inside Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and web browsers. Everything is checked on your phone, nothing it reads is stored or sent anywhere, and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings.

Block only Reels and Shorts on iPhone

iOS does not let one app look inside another, so Neurofast takes a different route on iPhone. It has a browser built in that opens Instagram, YouTube and TikTok with the Reels feed, Explore, the Shorts tab and the For You feed left out. A Reel or Short that a friend sends you opens as that one video, with no swiping to the next.

  1. Install Neurofast from the App Store.
  2. Block the Instagram, YouTube and TikTok apps with a daily limit or a full block.
  3. Use them from the browser inside Neurofast. Your messages, profiles and subscriptions are all there.

In the app this is called Social media without Reels. It is a Pro feature on iPhone too.

What to expect

Instagram, YouTube and TikTok change their apps often. After a big update Smart block can miss a screen until Neurofast is updated to match. If that happens, write to agilersoft@gmail.com and we will fix it.

Other apps can do part of this: AppBlock and one sec block Reels and Shorts on Android, and ScreenZen lists a similar mode for iPhone. They are compared in the best social media blocker apps.

If you only want less time in the whole app, you do not need Pro. A daily limit per app is free in Neurofast, and the block holds until midnight.
